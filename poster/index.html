Featured document

Musik des Mittelalters

This poster announced a four-part Freiburg series in April 1924 under the direction of the musicologist Wilibald Gurlitt. It is used on the homepage because it brings together the project’s central evidence: repertory, performance, scholarship, institutions, and historical ephemera.

Reading the poster

Across events on 1, 3, 5, and 8 April, the poster moves from a lecture on music and musical thought in the Gothic Middle Ages to three named programs: Musica ecclesiastica, Musica composita, and Musica vulgaris. Its typography makes visible how medieval music was framed simultaneously as a scholarly subject and as repertory for renewed performance.

Connected record

The database includes the surviving concert program for Musica ecclesiastica, 3 April 1924. The record places the historical program scan beside its standardized performance data.
